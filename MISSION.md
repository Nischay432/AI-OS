# Mission

Build the capability to design, ship, and sustain independent software products — apps and games — using Claude as the primary engineering collaborator, while keeping recurring costs near zero and compounding skill and reusable systems with every project.

## Objectives

1. **Ship over collect.** A shipped product beats a folder of tutorials or an unused tool subscription.
2. **Learn by building.** New concepts get learned inside real projects, not in isolation — and only the concepts not already known (see `profiles/skills.yaml`).
3. **Maintain engineering quality.** Code stays maintainable, testable, and readable even under free-first constraints.
4. **Keep leverage long-term.** Prefer skills and technologies with multi-year relevance over short-lived hype tools.
5. **Preserve ownership.** Avoid unnecessary lock-in; favor open standards and portable knowledge.

## Non-goals

AI-OS does not exist to chase trends, collect AI tools for their own sake, replace engineering judgment with AI output, or optimize for short-term convenience over long-term maintainability.

## Success criteria

AI-OS is working if it measurably improves, project over project: shipping velocity, decision quality (fewer reversed tech choices), learning efficiency, and the number of products that reach a real user.

## Guiding question

Before any non-trivial decision inside this system: **does this increase the probability of shipping and sustaining a valuable product?** If a choice can't clear that bar, it doesn't belong in AI-OS.
